06 · Google Ads

Google Ads: smart bidding from day one.

Maximize conversions from launch, with no CPA target. Daily budgets per market are the only spend limit.

LaunchMaximize conversionson sign-up, no CPA target
Weeks 1–2Learning periodno bid or structure changes
ThenOptimize inputssearch terms, negatives, budget by market
WhenStable FTD volumetracked with value
ThenMaximize conversion valueon FTD value, no ROAS target at first

Setup checklist

  • Geo by presence; one campaign set per market, budgets 30 / 35 / 35 (US / UK / Canada)
  • Primary conversion: sign-up. FTD in observation
  • Exact and phrase match, to keep queries tight with no CPA target
  • Negative list live from day 1
  • Ads run during market hours, Sunday to Friday
  • Daily search-term review in weeks 1–2
  • PMax: brand terms excluded, existing clients excluded, asset groups for gold, indices and crypto

Out of phase 1

  • Competitor terms
  • Demand Gen remarketing

AdRoll is the dedicated remarketing and re-engagement layer, for visitors and for the client base. PMax will also reach past visitors; that overlap is watched, not bought twice.

07 · AdRoll

AdRoll: find new traders, win back the ones who leave, wake up the base.

Half the plan, three jobs. Prospecting brings qualified Forex, crypto and CFD traders who aren’t searching yet. Remarketing and re-engagement work everyone GatesFX has already paid to acquire.

01 · US$2,000 · from day 1

Prospect qualified traders

Three ad groups, one per interest, each with its own creative and landing page. Contextual from day 1; lookalikes seeded from depositors from day 45.

  • Forex
  • Crypto
  • CFDs: gold, indices
KPI: cost per new visitor ≤ US$2*; sign-up rate by interest
02 · US$1,200 · from day 1

Remarket to visitors

Site visitors who didn’t register, and sign-ups who haven’t funded yet. Message moves from account type to first deposit.

  • Hot 0–7 days
  • Warm 8–30 days
KPI: sign-ups and FTDs, click-through
03 · US$800 · from day 1

Re-engage the client base

CRM lists synced daily: sign-ups that never funded after 30 days, dormant funded accounts, and active clients for redeposit and new products.

  • No deposit 30+ d
  • Dormant 30–90 d
  • Dormant 90+ d
  • Active
KPI: reactivated accounts, redeposits, cost per reactivation
≈16,500visitors a month needed to spend US$1,200 on retargeting*

gatesfx.com already gets ≈52k visits a month (Oct 2026), down from ≈83k in August. Paid adds about 1,600. The pool covers retargeting with room to spare; unique visitors will be lower than visits.

27% bounce, 12.5 pages and almost 6 minutes per visit point to heavy existing-client traffic. That favours re-engagement, and makes the 24 h depositor exclusion from acquisition segments essential.

Re-engagement draws on a CRM base of 100k+ emails, enough to support US$800 and more. The reachable audience depends on AdRoll’s match rate, read after the first upload.

Weekly rule: unspent retargeting or re-engagement moves to prospecting.

Setup checklist

  • Pixel on both domains
  • Daily CRM sync by status: sign-up, funded, last deposit and last trade date
  • Depositors leave acquisition segments within 24 h, enter re-engagement
  • Re-engagement only to clients with marketing consent
  • Separate creative per job: Forex, crypto and CFD for prospecting; deposit for retargeting; reactivation for the base
  • Budget on click-through; 1-day view-through reported apart
  • Viewability, IVT and brand-safety reporting; MFA excluded
  • Creative refreshed every 21 days
